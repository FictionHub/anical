Theatrical follow-up to Shibou Yuugi de Meshi wo Kuu. This is a story about a deranged world. Player name: Yuuki. Occupation: Professional death game player. Having overcome the jinx known as the "Wall of Thirty," which drastically reduces the survival rates of players, Yuuki continues her journey. Games where death is always close at hand. Her next challenge is on a remote island in the middle of the ocean. There, eight players have gathered. Among them are seasoned veterans whom Yuuki knows. The game – "Cloudy Beach." Beyond the wall, only those who have crossed the wall remain. Today, once again, she will play death games to put food on the table. (Source: Official Site, translated)
