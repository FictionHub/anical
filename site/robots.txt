# SEO-tool crawlers, blocked Aug 2026.

# These are not search engines. They crawl the site to build backlink and
# keyword databases they sell to their own subscribers; nothing they fetch ever
# sends a visitor here. On 12-13 Aug 2026 SemrushBot alone made ~180 requests
# from ~40 IPs across 85.208.96.0/24 and 185.191.171.0/24 — sixteen times what
# Googlebot did in the same day, and enough distinct addresses to be counted as
# ~40 separate "unique visitors" in Netlify Analytics.
#
# Two reasons to turn them away. The traffic is bought with nothing, and while
# they only touch static HTML today, a crawler that ever wanders onto /api/*
# burns metered function invocations. Blocking them also makes the analytics
# legible again, which is the reason this was noticed at all.
#
# All of these honour robots.txt. The scanners hammering /wp-admin/install.php
# do not and never will — they are answered by a 404 on a site with no PHP,
# which is the whole defence they need.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
